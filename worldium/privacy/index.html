Worldium Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Worldium game for Android (dev.imbrasas.worldium), made by Paulius Imbrasas ("I", "me").

In short: Worldium does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Your game is kept only on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Worldium has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no online features. It does not connect to the internet and asks for no device permissions.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your progress (your world, its settlements and your settings) in the app's private storage on your device. It also keeps a local log of game events used for balancing, which never leaves the device. None of this is sent to me or to anyone else.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Worldium, or clearing its storage in your device's app settings, deletes all of it. As I hold no data about you, there's nothing else to delete.

Third parties

Worldium includes no third-party services that collect data. If you install it from Google Play, Google processes information about the download and your device under Google's privacy policy; I receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics Google Play provides to every developer.

Children

As Worldium collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
