Varnelė Klaviatūra Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy covers the Varnelė Klaviatūra keyboard for Android (dev.imbrasas.varnele.keyboard), made by Paulius Imbrasas ("I", "me").

In short: everything you type stays on your phone. The keyboard has no internet access, so it can't send your text, the words it learns or anything else to me or to anyone else.

What the app collects

Nothing. Varnelė Klaviatūra has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no online features. It does not ask Android for internet access, so it cannot connect to any server. Spelling, grammar, autocorrect, swipe typing and word predictions all run on your device.

What stays on your device

A keyboard sees what you type. To do its job, it keeps some of that in its private storage on your phone:

  • Words you write. With "Learn from my writing" on (it's on by default), the keyboard counts the words and word pairs you use, so its predictions get better for you. It also remembers words you type often, as most keyboards do. It does not learn in incognito mode, in password fields, in fields that ask keyboards not to learn, or from web or e-mail addresses.
  • Your personal dictionary. Words you add yourself. This is Android's own dictionary, so other keyboards on your phone can use it too.
  • Clipboard history. Text you've copied, so you can paste it again. You can turn it off in the keyboard's settings.
  • Your settings, such as layouts, theme and correction options.
  • Crash reports. If the keyboard crashes, it saves a technical report (app version, device model, error details) on your device. It's only sent anywhere if you choose to share it yourself.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional, off by default): only if you turn on contact name suggestions. Names are read to suggest them while you type and never leave the device.
  • Personal dictionary (read and write): to suggest your own words and let you add new ones.
  • Vibration: for feedback when you press a key.
  • Run at startup: so the keyboard is ready after your phone restarts.

Deleting your data

In the keyboard's Varnelė settings, "Forget learned words" deletes the word and word-pair counts learned from your writing. Clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it. As I hold no data about you, there's nothing else to delete.

Backups

If you have Android's backup to your Google account turned on, Android may include the keyboard's settings and learned words in that backup. It is stored by Google under your account, not by me, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Third parties

Varnelė Klaviatūra includes no third-party services that collect data. If you install it from Google Play, Google processes information about the download and your device under Google's privacy policy; I receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics Google Play provides to every developer.

Children

As Varnelė Klaviatūra collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app collects, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
