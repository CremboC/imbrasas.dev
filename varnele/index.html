Varnelė Klaviatūra

A Lithuanian keyboard for Android that fixes as you type.

Type without diacritics and in everyday spoken Lithuanian: Varnelė adds the missing letters, fixes typos and grammar (case, agreement, commas) and predicts the next word. Swipe typing included. It's based on the open-source HeliBoard keyboard.
